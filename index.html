Cross-device workspace
Google Drive sync
Your papers and progress stay in a visible folder in your own Drive.
One-time setup: create a Google OAuth Web application client ID in Google Cloud Console, enable the Google Drive API, and add the exact website origin to Authorized JavaScript origins. This app requests the drive.file permission and creates only its own files.
The client ID is public configuration, not a client secret. Never paste a client secret here.
Not connected. Your current data remains saved in this browser.
What syncs: question bank additions, custom mocks, paper dates and names, attempts, answer images, faculty marks, feedback, tracker data and settings. A single account-wide data file is stored in My Drive / NID Prep Lab.
Important: connect and complete the first upload on the device that currently contains your latest mocks. Then connect the same Google account on your other devices. Avoid editing on two devices simultaneously; use Sync now after switching devices.
